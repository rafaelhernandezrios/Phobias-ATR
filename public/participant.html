Informed consent

This session involves exposure to immersive 360° visual content that may include spiders, snakes, insects, heights, or enclosed spaces. Intensity increases gradually and always under the researcher's supervision.

  • You may stop the session at any time.
  • During exposure an EMERGENCY EXIT button is always visible.
  • If you wear the AURA EEG headset, data is linked to an anonymizable experiment_id.
  • If you feel significant discomfort, remove the headset and notify the researcher.

By pressing Accept you agree to participate under these conditions.
